Empirical Severity Risk Index ($S_{emp} \in [5, 100]$)

Severity is calculated objectively without making unsubstantiated depth claims:
S = W_cat + S_area + S_prox + Δ_temporal
Where W_cat is category hazard weight, S_area is visible bounding box area fraction, S_prox is perspective proximity in the lane travel path, and Δ_temporal is tracking persistence bonus/discount.
